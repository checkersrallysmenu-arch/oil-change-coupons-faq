Do oil change coupons really work, or is it a gimmick?

They really work. The big chains publish coupons on their own websites all year because oil changes are how they win repeat customers. The catch is that most offers are regional. A deal that scans in Texas may not exist in Oregon, which is why generic coupon sites frustrate people. The reliable route is the brand's own coupon page for your zip code, starting with Jiffy Lube's official coupons.
