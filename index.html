Do oil change coupons really work, or is it a gimmick?

They really work. The big chains publish coupons on their own websites all year because oil changes are how they win repeat customers. The catch is that most coupons are regional. A code that scans in Texas may not exist in Oregon, which is why generic coupon sites frustrate people. On Oil Change Coupon Codes every listing notes where it works and when it expires, because we check the official sources ourselves.

What is the best Jiffy Lube deal right now?

It depends on your state. Some franchise groups run a $30 off Jiffy Lube coupon, others run percentage deals, and a few regions publish 50% off. The strongest widely available offer is usually $10 to $15 off, so a $30 code is worth grabbing when your local group offers one. How does Take 5 handle coupons?

Differently from everyone else. Take 5 does not do printable promo codes. Its standing deal is $15 off when you join its text or email list, and the coupon is shown on your phone in the shop. The full breakdown of how it works is on our Take 5 coupon page, along with its military and rideshare discounts.

Why did a coupon site show me a code that did not scan?

Usually because the code was invented, expired, or copied from another region. Aggregator sites rarely check anything.
